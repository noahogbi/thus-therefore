The algorithm has three phases:

- factor the modulus into primes
- solve the congruence for each prime power
- combine the solutions with the CRT

Applied to our case:

* the modulus 45 factors as 9 * 5
* modulo 9 the solution is x = 4
* modulo 5 the solution is x = 2

Numbered recap for the writeup:

1. factor 45
2. solve both congruences
3. combine

Combining x = 4 (mod 9) and x = 2 (mod 5) gives x = 22 (mod 45).

ANSWER: 22
