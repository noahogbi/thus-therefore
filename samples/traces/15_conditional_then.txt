If x is even, then x*x is divisible by 4. If x is odd, then x*x leaves
remainder 1 modulo 8.

Our x is 6, which is even. Then, by the first rule, 36 is divisible by 4.
Check: 36 = 4 * 9.

If we had started from x = 7 instead, then the second rule would apply, and
indeed 49 = 48 + 1 leaves remainder 1 modulo 8.

Thus, the divisibility claim holds for x = 6.

ANSWER: yes
