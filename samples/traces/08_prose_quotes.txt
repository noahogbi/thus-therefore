The problem cites the classical fact: "If a prime divides a product, then it
divides one of the factors." This is Euclid's lemma.

Note that 7 divides 84, since 84 = 7 * 12. The problem asks whether 7 divides
21 * 4.

Observe that 21 * 4 = 84. So the answer follows directly: 7 divides 21 * 4, and
by the quoted lemma 7 must divide 21 or divide 4. Indeed 21 = 7 * 3.

The hint said "Thus, use the lemma directly," and that is what we did.

ANSWER: yes
