The map is g(x) = 3*x - 5, applied twice to x = -4.

First application: g(-4) = 3*(-4) - 5 = -12 - 5 = -17.

Second application: g(-17) = 3*(-17) - 5 = -51 - 5 = -56.

Note that the sign stays negative throughout, since each step multiplies a
negative value by a positive constant and then subtracts 5.

Hence the result is -56.

ANSWER: -56
